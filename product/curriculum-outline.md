# Original curriculum outline: first English sounds and words

**Inferred — original proposal.** This sequence is authored for Vietnamese-speaking children aged 3–6 who cannot read English. It is a content plan, not a proven teaching sequence. No third-party lesson order, script, art, or audio is used. A parent may replay any lesson. Each lesson should be brief enough for a child to finish with one spoken goal and a small picture check; actual duration and comprehension are **Unknown** until family testing.

The child first hears and recognizes whole spoken words in familiar scenes. Later lessons draw attention to initial sounds; letters appear only after the child has heard and used the words. Early reading is a later extension, not a prerequisite to completing oral units. Vietnamese voice guides actions while an original English recording models each target. Assess recognition by picture choice, never by asking a pre-reader to decode an instruction.

Step names below are the types in the [lesson engine specification](lesson-engine-spec.md#typed-step-shapes): `model`, `errorless`, `check`, `media`, `celebrate`. They name the steps only; the engine specification owns their behavior. A `check` uses familiar distractors from prior lessons. Each listed sound is an **attention cue**, not a pronunciation or mastery claim. `—` means no letter is shown.

| Unit and goal | Lesson | Target words (original selection) | Sound / letter focus | Step types |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Things I can hold**: hear and choose concrete objects | 1.1 At the play mat | ball, cup | Listen to the beginning of *ball*; letter — | `model`, `errorless`, `check`, `celebrate` |
| | 1.2 Getting ready | bag, hat | Listen to the beginning of *bag*; letter — | `model`, `errorless`, `check`, `celebrate` |
| | 1.3 Find it again | ball, cup, bag, hat | Hear the four whole words again; letter — | `model`, `check`, `celebrate` |
| **2. My small garden**: expand oral words in an everyday scene | 2.1 By the window | sun, rain | Notice different first sounds; letter — | `model`, `errorless`, `check`, `celebrate` |
| | 2.2 In the pot | leaf, seed | Listen for the first sound in *sun* and *seed*; letter — | `model`, `errorless`, `check`, `celebrate` |
| | 2.3 Garden choices | sun, rain, leaf, seed | Choose a heard word among familiar pictures; letter — | `model`, `check`, `celebrate` |
| **3. Sounds I know**: connect known words that start alike | 3.1 The /b/ pair | ball, bag | Hear /b/ in both known words; show **B/b** after oral review | `model`, `errorless`, `check`, `celebrate` |
| | 3.2 The /s/ pair | sun, seed | Hear /s/ in both known words; show **S/s** after oral review | `model`, `errorless`, `check`, `celebrate` |
| | 3.3 Listen and choose | ball, bag, sun, seed | Choose the known word, then distinguish /b/ and /s/; **B/b**, **S/s** | `model`, `check`, `celebrate` |
| **4. A tiny spoken scene**: use known words in context | 4.1 Pack for outside | bag, hat, sun | Hear a short original spoken scene and select the named picture; **B/b**, **S/s** are optional visual reminders | `model`, `media`, `check`, `celebrate` |
| | 4.2 After the rain | rain, leaf, cup | Hear a short original spoken scene and select the named picture; no new letter | `model`, `media`, `check`, `celebrate` |
| | 4.3 My picture trail | ball, bag, sun, seed, rain, leaf | Review oral recognition across scenes; optional **B/b**, **S/s** sound association | `model`, `check`, `celebrate` |

## Publishing and progression notes

- **Inferred:** Units 1–2 are the initial MVP content. Unit 3 is a later sound–letter path after oral recognition is usable; unit 4 requires original media assets and belongs to the `Should` scope in [requirements](mvp-requirements.md#features-not-yet-verified).
- **Inferred:** A lesson may be replayed freely. A finished lesson records participation; first-answer results on `check` steps are kept separately. There is no timed test, streak, penalty, or claim of mastery from completion.
- **Unknown:** Which words are familiar to a particular family, whether the /b/ and /s/ pairings are clear across English recordings for Vietnamese children, and the appropriate number of choices by age. Validate with native English voice review, Vietnamese-speaking caregivers, early-years educators, and observed child sessions. Revise the original word set if those tests reveal confusion.
