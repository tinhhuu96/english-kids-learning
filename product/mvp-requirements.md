# Preschool English MVP requirements

## Scope and evidence

This is an **Inferred — original product proposal**, not a validated learning intervention. The child is 3–6, speaks Vietnamese, and cannot yet read English. A parent starts the experience, manages settings, and reviews practice. The child's path must work without reading instructions or supplying credentials. The first release teaches listening and recognition of a small original English vocabulary; letters and reading follow oral familiarity.

Evidence labels describe the **research behind a decision**, not proof that our proposed feature works: **Confirmed** = direct runtime/REA observation or a public page (a publisher statement is confirmed only as a statement); **Inferred** = a conclusion from evidence, including anything read only from static code; **Unknown** = not verified. All acceptance criteria below are proposed build checks. See the [feature inventory](../research/feature-inventory.md), [reference comparison](../research/reference-comparison.md), and [technical evidence](../research/technical-evidence.md) for source boundaries. The [curriculum outline](curriculum-outline.md) supplies original content; the [lesson engine specification](lesson-engine-spec.md) defines step behavior.

## Features researched and confirmed

The cited pattern was directly seen or publicly described. Adopting it for Vietnamese preschoolers remains an **Inferred** design decision.

| Priority | Proposed feature and acceptance criteria | Research evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Must** | **Audio-led lesson entry.** A large Play action unlocks sound. Each step offers a Vietnamese spoken guide and a separate English target recording; either can be replayed. A child can complete the first lesson without reading text. | **Confirmed:** Play gates were observed in the [Starfall activity samples](../research/feature-inventory.md#cross-activity-patterns). **Inferred — from static code:** the pilot and sound–letter game load spoken step cues and target clips. **Unknown:** whether our wording is understood by children. |
| **Must** | **Short, ordered lessons.** Each published lesson has one listening goal, begins with `model`, places any `errorless` exploration before `check`, and has a clear end. Next stays unavailable until the current step finishes; Back replays without a second score. | **Confirmed:** the sound–letter sample showed an introduction and a checked choice; Duolingo ABC publicly describes short lessons ([comparison](../research/reference-comparison.md#duolingo-abc--early-literacy-and-phonics)). **Inferred — from static code:** later pilot/book navigation is gated. |
| **Must** | **Touch recognition check with calm retry.** A child can answer by tapping one of two or three pictures. A wrong answer keeps the item open, offers replay, and never removes a reward; an outside drag, if drag is offered, is not scored. Completion and first-answer accuracy are recorded separately. | **Confirmed:** one wrong and one right tile were exercised in the [sound–letter sample](../research/feature-inventory.md#activity-p--make-a-word-with--at-soundletter-game). **Inferred:** tap-only access and gentle feedback are our design choices; the sample's full reward path was not observed. |
| **Must** | **Phone-sized, accessible controls.** Core actions remain usable at 390×844 portrait and landscape; interactive targets render at least 64 CSS px. Every actionable picture has a spoken name, visible focus, keyboard action, and a tap equivalent for drag. Reduced-motion preference suppresses nonessential animation. | **Confirmed:** a sampled reading canvas shrank to 390×292 in portrait and lacked semantic canvas choices ([inventory](../research/feature-inventory.md#activity-r--zac-the-rat-learn-to-read-lesson)). **Inferred:** DOM/SVG controls, 64 px, and equivalent actions are proposed remedies, not measured child usability. |
| **Should** | **Parent-only practice summary.** After a lesson, a parent can see finished lessons, words practised, and first-answer recognition attempts; participation is never labelled mastery. Child screens show only a simple completion picture and offer replay. | **Confirmed:** Lingokids publicly describes a parent Progress Center, and Duolingo describes path progress ([comparison](../research/reference-comparison.md)). **Unknown:** their underlying child records and our summary's usefulness. |

## Features not yet verified

These features have **Unknown** product or child-use evidence. The checks below define what would verify each need before release or promotion in priority.

| Priority | Proposed feature and acceptance criteria | Evidence gap and verification |
|---|---|---|
| **Must** | **Vietnamese voice quality.** Every child-facing instruction, retry cue, and parent-exit cue has an original Vietnamese recording; English target pronunciation is recorded separately. In a moderated test, a child can start, replay, and answer without an adult reading the screen. | **Unknown:** no research participant listened to the sample audio or tested our wording. Verify with Vietnamese-speaking families across ages 3–6 and review recordings with a language educator. |
| **Must** | **Resumable progress.** Reopen an interrupted lesson at the last completed step without duplicate rewards or attempts. A parent can reset a child profile. | **Unknown:** per-child persistence in sampled products was not established. Verify with offline/reload/sync tests and parent task testing; see the [engine event contract](lesson-engine-spec.md#state-machines-and-scoring). |
| **Should** | **Offline lesson pack.** A parent can explicitly download at least one complete non-video unit; the child can open, finish, and resume it with the network off. The UI shows pack size and failed/incomplete download state. | **Confirmed: publisher statement:** Duolingo ABC's store listing says offline play is available ([comparison](../research/reference-comparison.md#duolingo-abc--early-literacy-and-phonics)). **Unknown:** its mechanics and device capacity. Verify on low-storage Android and iOS devices, including interrupted downloads. |
| **Should** | **Bounded hints.** After inactivity, a visual cue appears; after repeated misses, the Vietnamese hint can replay once. Hints pause during narration and are never required to proceed. | **Confirmed:** a pilot visual idle hint was seen. **Inferred — from static code:** timing and repeat limits in that product. **Unknown:** effective timing for our learners. Verify with family observation and tune the timing before launch. |
| **Should** | **Original song or story step.** A parent can play/pause/replay a short original media item; captions and a parent transcript accompany it. Playback alone never increments recognition accuracy. | **Confirmed:** public song player controls and a reading intro were observed ([inventory](../research/feature-inventory.md)). **Inferred — from static code:** later reading flow and media tracking semantics. **Unknown:** our content's comprehension; verify with a child listening task and accessibility review. |
| **Later** | **Letter and early reading path.** Unlock letter/sound association after oral word recognition. A child can hear a sound, choose its letter, and hear it again; no spelling or decoding score is inferred from tapping through a story. | **Confirmed:** a sampled sound–letter game checked a wrong/right answer. **Unknown:** readiness threshold for Vietnamese preschoolers. Verify sequence and tasks with early-years educators and child sessions before expanding beyond the outline. |
| **Later** | **Multiple child profiles and caregiver controls.** A parent can switch profiles and set a session limit; one child's records never appear in another's summary. | **Confirmed: publisher statement:** Lingokids describes profiles and screen-time controls ([comparison](../research/reference-comparison.md#lingokids--preschool-curriculum-and-learning-experience)). **Unknown:** demand and suitable settings for our families; verify through parent interviews and privacy review. |

## Non-functional release requirements

These are **Inferred — provisional targets** for our product, except where evidence is identified. They require measurement on our implementation and real devices.

| Area | Acceptance criterion and verification |
|---|---|
| **Load budget** | At 390×844 on a low-end Android device over a throttled mobile connection, the Play action becomes usable within 5 seconds and transfers at most 500 KB before first interaction, excluding an optional media download. Measure encoded bytes and elapsed time across at least five cold loads; record median and worst run. **Confirmed context:** sampled external activities transferred about 1.1–1.35 MB before their Play/game state in single desktop runs ([measurements](../research/technical-evidence.md#fallback-trigger-commands-and-performance-definition)). This is a target, not a proven comparison. |
| **Offline** | The installed shell, one parent-downloaded lesson pack, and unsynced attempts survive a reload with the network off. Sync retries use stable event IDs and do not duplicate progress. A failed pack cannot appear complete. |
| **Accessibility** | Child and parent flows support keyboard, visible focus, spoken alternatives for pictures, semantic controls, captions/transcripts for media, and reduced motion. Test portrait/landscape and screen readers on actual devices; do not claim WCAG conformance from source inspection alone. |
| **Child privacy** | No ads, public profile, social features, child email, microphone, or third-party tracking in the child path. Store only a parent-managed nickname/age band and pseudonymous progress needed for lessons; allow parent export and deletion. Validate data flows and deletion before release. |
| **Vietnamese UI voice** | Child guidance and feedback use natural Vietnamese audio; English is reserved for target words/sounds. A replay control is always reachable. Text is available for caregivers and accessibility, without being necessary for the child's task. |

No educational efficacy, ideal duration, mastery threshold, offline capacity, or real-device audio reliability has been established by this research. Those remain **Unknown** until the verification above is complete.
