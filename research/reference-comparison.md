## Method and evidence

I read the four Starfall pilot files before editing this comparison. For each secondary web page below, I ran REA `capture-browser-scenario` in a fresh headless Chrome session with no cookies or login, a 200 ms settle step, and end-of-step DOM and URL capture. Raw JSON is in the run scratch directory only. The REA evidence IDs document successful captures; a DOM capture of a marketing page establishes the published claim, not the in-app interaction. No third-party media or lesson text was committed.

The exact REA invocation was `rea capture-browser-scenario "$scenario" --format json`, where `scenario` was the following compact JSON with the listed URL substituted for `URL` (the first Lingokids exploratory capture used the same fields without `capture` and a 1000 ms wait):

```json
{"browser":{"mode":"launch","executable_path":"/usr/bin/google-chrome"},"start_url":{"url":"URL"},"actions":[{"step_id":"observe","action":"wait_for_timeout","duration_ms":200}],"capture":{"at_end":["dom","url"]}}
```

## Product evidence and MVP implications

### Lingokids — preschool curriculum and learning experience

- **Lesson structure / flow.** [Confirmed] Its structured Lessons area is described as an initial assessment, 8–10 video/game units, then review and a final assessment, spread over multiple sessions. The announced subjects include literacy, math, and English language learning at preschool, kindergarten, and first-grade readiness levels ([Lingokids Lessons announcement](https://lingokids.com/press/new-lingokids-feature-proves-90-effective-in-improving-childrens-skill-learning-abilities)). [Confirmed] The broader curriculum offers songs, stories, videos, and interactive activities across many topics ([curriculum](https://lingokids.com/playlearning-curriculum)). **Recommendation:** [Inferred] For ages 3–6, use a much smaller unit: one spoken goal, two or three play steps, then a gentle check, repeatable over short sessions.
- **Audio / instructions.** [Confirmed] Songs, podcasts, and listening skills appear in the public curriculum ([curriculum](https://lingokids.com/playlearning-curriculum)). [Unknown] The exact spoken directions, voice language, replay control, and audio timing inside a game are unknown. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Give the child a Vietnamese spoken instruction and separate English target audio, both replayable from large icons.
- **Right/wrong feedback.** [Confirmed] The published lesson flow includes diagnostic and final assessments ([announcement](https://lingokids.com/press/new-lingokids-feature-proves-90-effective-in-improving-childrens-skill-learning-abilities)). [Unknown] Its response to a correct or incorrect tap, retry rules, and whether every game even grades answers are unknown. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Use immediate visual and sound acknowledgement, then a calm replay and hint after a miss.
- **Progress / rewards.** [Confirmed] The Parents Area describes a Progress Center after five activities, showing activity counts and favored subjects or skills ([Parents Area](https://lingokids.com/why-lingokids/parents-area)). [Unknown] Child-facing points, collectibles, and completion animations were not verified. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Show a simple completed-picture trail for the child; give parents counts and concepts practised.
- **Parent features / login boundary.** [Confirmed] The Parents Area describes screen-time controls, profiles, and switches for types of content; its page says multiple child profiles are a Plus feature ([Parents Area](https://lingokids.com/why-lingokids/parents-area)). [Unknown] The precise account wall, purchase flow, parent gate, and what an unauthenticated child can open were not exercised. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Put settings and reports behind an adult gate, with no child-facing subscription prompt.
- **Technical / observable data.** [Inferred] The published sequence suggests conceptual entities `lesson → unit → activity → assessment` and `child → completed activity → practised skill`; these are design abstractions, **not** observed app records. [Unknown] Storage, scoring formula, offline caching, and per-step events are unknown. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Store typed step and response records under a parent-managed child profile, with local cache for interrupted sessions.

### Duolingo ABC — early literacy and phonics

- **Lesson structure / flow.** [Confirmed] The teaching page says lessons take five minutes or less and learners can begin at one of nine levels; its curriculum covers phonics, phonemic awareness, comprehension, fluency, and vocabulary ([how we teach](https://abc.duolingo.com/how-we-teach)). [Confirmed: publisher statement] The store listing describes tracing, drag-and-drop activities, and interactive stories ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/learn-to-read-duolingo-abc/id1440502568)). **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Start with sound recognition and a small tap or drag practice; defer English reading and spelling until children have the oral vocabulary.
- **Audio / instructions.** [Confirmed] The teaching page describes stories with narration and reading along with a narrator ([how we teach](https://abc.duolingo.com/how-we-teach)). [Unknown] In-game instruction language, exact prompts, and narration replay controls are unknown. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Keep Vietnamese guidance separate from English sounds and use a replay button without requiring reading.
- **Right/wrong feedback.** [Confirmed] The curriculum explicitly includes interactive questions within stories ([how we teach](https://abc.duolingo.com/how-we-teach)). [Unknown] Exact correct/wrong screens, retry policy, and whether mistakes block progression are not established by the public page or store listing. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Allow a second try, then demonstrate the answer with audio and invite a low-pressure retry.
- **Progress / rewards.** [Confirmed: publisher statement] The store listing says mini games and rewards motivate children and that lessons follow a learning sequence ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/learn-to-read-duolingo-abc/id1440502568)). [Unknown] The exact reward icon, currency, progress visualization, and scoring formula were not verified. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Celebrate completion of a concept rather than speed or a daily streak.
- **Parent features / login boundary.** [Confirmed] Duolingo's ABC privacy notice says a parent sets up the app, can control settings and delete app data from an adult-oriented screen, and that an email is optional during registration ([ABC privacy](https://www.duolingo.com/abc-privacy)). [Unknown] The exact adult gate and availability of a parent progress report were not verified. [Unknown] The mobile onboarding and any feature boundary after setup remain unknown because no account or app was used. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Ask the adult for only a child nickname and age band, then make learning accessible without child credentials.
- **Technical / observable data.** [Confirmed] The public method describes nine starting levels and a cumulative path ([how we teach](https://abc.duolingo.com/how-we-teach)). [Inferred] A conceptual `level → lesson → activity/story` structure follows from that description; it is not an observed payload. [Unknown] Local progress format, audio assets, and telemetry are unknown. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Represent each item as a typed content record with audio IDs, expected responses, and a recorded completion state.

### Duolingo — progress and reward patterns

- **Lesson structure / flow.** [Confirmed] Duolingo describes units of short lessons arranged on a learning path, with exercises that become more demanding and later review of earlier material ([Duolingo guide](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-101-how-to-learn-a-language-on-duolingo/)). **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Use a visual route of completed concepts, but let a parent revisit any completed activity.
- **Audio / instructions.** [Confirmed] Its public guide lists listening and speaking exercises, speaker controls in illustrated exercises, and stories with audio in some courses ([Duolingo guide](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-101-how-to-learn-a-language-on-duolingo/)). [Unknown] The exact language and audio behavior for a particular course or anonymous web session are unknown. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Put all child directions in spoken Vietnamese and let them hear English targets on demand.
- **Right/wrong feedback.** [Confirmed] The public guide says practice lessons target earlier mistakes; Duolingo describes grading answers as correct or incorrect after checking an exercise ([Duolingo guide](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-101-how-to-learn-a-language-on-duolingo/), [feedback/reporting article](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-user-reports-improve-course-content/)). [Unknown] Exact animations and penalty behavior can vary by current course and subscription and were not tested. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Keep immediate correction but remove penalties for preschool learners.
- **Progress / rewards.** [Confirmed] The guide describes path progress, XP for completed learning actions, a day-based streak, and a course score; the achievements article describes milestone badges and personal records ([Duolingo guide](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-101-how-to-learn-a-language-on-duolingo/), [achievements](https://blog.duolingo.com/achievement-badges/)). **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Celebrate finished lessons and mastered sounds privately; avoid daily streak pressure, league ranking, and exchangeable currency for ages 3–6.
- **Parent features / login boundary.** [Confirmed] The guide mentions friends and family features and says a profile saves progress across devices ([Duolingo guide](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-101-how-to-learn-a-language-on-duolingo/)). [Unknown] A preschool-specific parent dashboard was not established by these sources. [Unknown] How many lessons an anonymous web visitor can finish and which rewards require login were not tested. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Provide a parent-only summary of recent practice and sound recognition, without social features.
- **Technical / observable data.** [Inferred] The public UI implies conceptual entities `course → unit → path node → exercise` plus `learner → completion / XP / streak / badge`; these are **not** observed API schemas. [Unknown] Storage, scoring rules, and reward-event payloads remain unknown. **Recommendation:** [Inferred] Track concept mastery separately from rewards so reward tuning cannot corrupt learning progress.

## What we adopt / adapt / avoid for a Vietnamese preschool MVP

- **Adopt:** [Inferred] A large start control, short audio-led steps, immediate response to each action, and a visible completion point. Record each step as content data rather than as a fixed animation timeline.
- **Adapt:** [Inferred] Use the path idea as a small picture trail that shows *concepts learned*, with a parent-controlled replay. Use separate Vietnamese instruction audio and English target audio; do not require the child to read instructions.
- **Adapt:** [Inferred] Use an easy first try for exploration, then a gentle recognition check. On a miss, replay the sound, give a visual cue, and allow retry; avoid errorless practice as the only assessment.
- **Adopt:** [Inferred] A parent-only view with activity counts, recently practised sounds, and an age-appropriate session limit. Let the child start a cached activity without account interactions on the child screen.
- **Avoid:** [Inferred] Daily streaks, public competition, loss of attempts, shop currency, ads, and subscription prompts in the child flow. These reward attendance or spending rather than listening and understanding.
- **Avoid:** [Inferred] Any copied brand names, characters, illustrations, sound files, lesson text, reward wording, or visual layout. Commission original art and voice work and write original Vietnamese prompts.
